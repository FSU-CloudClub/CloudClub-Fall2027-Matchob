AWS documents that presigned URLs grant bearer access and can be reused until expiration: [Download and upload objects with presigned URLs](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/userguide/using-presigned-url.html). The P3-4 and P7-4 conclusions combine those semantics with this repository's direct S3 signing and deletion order; they are not results of a live AWS test.
